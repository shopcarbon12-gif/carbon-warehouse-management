## Product Overview
**One-liner:** CARBON is a streetwear-led fashion brand for men and women, sold in its Orlando store and on shopcarbon.com.
**What it does:** CARBON designs and sells its own private-label clothing: denim, matching sets, dresses, tops, tees, sweatshirts, outerwear, shoes and accessories. The pieces are trend-forward and priced for everyday wear. Shoppers buy in person at the Edgewater store in Orlando, Florida, or online with shipping to the US and Canada.
**Product category:** Men's and women's fashion / streetwear. Shoppers search by garment ("men's ripped jeans", "women's mini dress", "matching sweat set"), not by brand.
**Product type:** Retail and e-commerce (DTC private label), one physical store plus Shopify. The POS is Carbon POS (Shopify POS channel unused). Stock is tracked by RFID in the CarbonWMS.
**Business model:** One-time purchases. 597 active products. Typical prices:
- Men's jeans $88–138 (median ~$100); women's jeans $63–87
- Dresses $44–117 (median $79); women's tops $21–71 (median $45)
- Tees $39–68; sweatshirts $43–90; men's pants $65–95; shorts $38–67
- Shoes $20–99; accessories $14–32

**Loyalty:** CARBON Rewards. 10 points = $1, redeemed in 100-point steps, capped at 50% of an order. 5,434 members hold points. Rewards emails are currently switched OFF.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem (owner, 2026-10-24):** At the big chains everyone buys the same pieces. "You go to a party, you get to the club, and a few people are wearing the same shirt as you."
**Why alternatives fall short:** Mall and chain stores sell the same high-volume styles to everyone. Cheaper fast fashion also looks and wears cheap.
**What it costs them:** The night out loses its point: they arrive and look like everyone else.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting to stand out and feel confident when they arrive somewhere. This is the AW2026 campaign idea ("Every Entrance Makes a Statement").

## Competitive Landscape
*Internal only. Never name or compare to any of these in copy (owner rule 2026-10-19).*
**Direct:** mall and chain fashion stores: Zara, Express, Mania Jeans and similar. They fall short because the same piece is on everyone and nothing feels personal.
**Secondary:** online fast fashion. Cheap, but the quality shows and the styles are everywhere.
**Indirect:** designer and luxury labels. Distinctive, but priced far above CARBON.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators (owner, 2026-10-24):**
- **Boutique, not chain:** small runs, so you won't walk into a party wearing the same shirt as three other people
- **European, on-trend styling:** fashion-forward looks you don't see at the big chains
- **A step up in quality:** most products are made in Turkey, not mass-produced in China *(internal fact; see the copy rules below)*
- **Complete looks:** matching sets, denim, shoes and accessories in one place
- **Try it on in person** at the Orlando store

**How we do it differently:** Boutique buying of trend-led European styles in small quantities, sold at mid-range prices.
**Why that's better:** You get a look that feels current and personal, at a price below designer.
**Why customers choose us (owner's words):** "It's trendy, it's fashionable, it's European fashion, a different style, with slightly higher quality … and not everyone wears the same thing. When you buy from me, you buy boutique."

**Copy rules for these points (open questions for the owner):**
- **Origin:** "Made in Turkey" is a real reason to buy, but the standing rule (2026-10-19) says copy never states where a product is from. Until the owner says otherwise, don't use origin.
- **Quality:** "Higher quality" is a selling point, but product pages ban "premium/high quality" claims. Use it only in brand copy, if the owner approves, and show it through visible details (fabric, finish) rather than claiming it.
- **Never "not made in China":** it's a negative comparison.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Will it fit?" (online) | Every product page shows the model's height and the size worn. Exchange within 14 days. First exchange label is free. |
| "Returns?" | Exchange or store credit only, within 14 days, unworn with tags. **No cash refunds online.** Never imply otherwise. |
| "Shipping cost" | Free standard shipping on $125+ to the US and Canada. |
| "A bit expensive" ($100 jeans) | The main objection (owner). It has eased since the online flash sale (10/20/30%). Answer with the boutique story, not by discounting deeper: you won't see it on everyone, and it's European styling a step above chain quality. |

**Anti-persona:** Shoppers outside the US/Canada (no shipping). Bargain hunters looking only for the lowest price (CARBON prices are mid-range, not discount).

## Goals
**Business goal:** Grow sales after leaving the malls. Mall foot traffic is gone (owner: "this is a problem"). Now there is one Orlando warehouse store and the website. The binding constraint is **traffic**, not conversion: about 19 real online sessions/day, ~0.10% online conversion.
**Biggest asset:** ~9,500 past customers, 3 in 4 of them outside Orlando, many holding unspent Rewards. Bringing them back online is cheaper than cold ads, which failed.
**Conversion actions:** (1) store visit at Edgewater, (2) online purchase, (3) email signup.
